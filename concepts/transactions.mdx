In MoneyDev, a **payment** represents the customer-facing event — the act of a customer paying you. A **transaction** is the underlying financial record that MoneyDev creates on your behalf when money actually moves. A single payment typically generates at least one transaction (a credit to your balance), and possibly more (e.g. a separate fee debit or a refund credit). Understanding the distinction helps you reconcile your balance accurately and forecast cash flow.

## Settlement Timelines

Settlement is the process by which paid funds become available in your MoneyDev balance after clearing through the Brazilian payment networks. Each payment method has its own settlement window:

| Method | Settlement Timeline | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **PIX** | D+1 | Funds received 24/7; credited to your balance on the next business day. |
| **Boleto** | D+2 after payment | Settlement starts after the bank confirms payment, which can take up to one business day. |
| **Credit Card** | D+30 (standard) | Based on the transaction date, not the statement date. Anticipation is available — see below. |
| **Debit Card** | D+1 | Online authorization required; settled the next business day. |

<Info>
**Receivables Anticipation** lets you access credit card revenue before the standard D+30 window — often as fast as D+2 — for a small anticipation fee. You can request anticipation for individual receivables or your full receivables schedule directly from the MoneyDev Dashboard under **Financeiro → Antecipação**, or via the Anticipation API endpoint. Check your contract for the applicable anticipation rate.
</Info>

## Transaction Object

<ResponseField name="id" type="string" required>
  Unique identifier for the transaction. Format: `txn_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="payment_id" type="string" required>
  The ID of the payment that originated this transaction. Use this to cross-reference transactions with their source payment.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="type" type="string" required>
  Direction of the money movement relative to your balance. Either `credit` (money coming in) or `debit` (money going out, e.g. refunds or fees).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="amount" type="integer" required>
  Gross transaction amount in centavos, before fees are deducted.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="net_amount" type="integer" required>
  Amount actually credited or debited to your balance in centavos, after all fees are deducted. This is the number that affects your available balance.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="fee" type="integer" required>
  Total fee charged by MoneyDev for this transaction, in centavos. Equal to `amount - net_amount` for credit transactions.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="currency" type="string" required>
  Always `BRL`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string" required>
  Settlement status of the transaction. One of: `pending` (not yet settled), `settled` (funds available in balance), `cancelled` (transaction voided, e.g. due to a chargeback reversal).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="settled_at" type="string">
  ISO 8601 timestamp of when the transaction was settled and funds became available in your balance. `null` if still pending.
</ResponseField>

## Accessing Your Transaction History

### MoneyDev Dashboard

The easiest way to view your transactions is through the Dashboard:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the Dashboard">
    Go to [dashboard.moneydev.com.br](https://dashboard.moneydev.com.br) and sign in with your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Transactions">
    Click **Financeiro** in the left sidebar, then select **Transações**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and export">
    Use the date range, payment method, and status filters to narrow results. Click **Exportar CSV** to download a statement for reconciliation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Transactions API

For programmatic access, query the Transactions endpoint directly:

```bash
curl https://api.moneydev.com.br/v1/transactions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SECRET_KEY" \
  -G \
  --data-urlencode "status=settled" \
  --data-urlencode "created_after=2024-08-01T00:00:00Z" \
  --data-urlencode "limit=50"
```

The response is a paginated list of transaction objects. Use the `next_cursor` value from the response to fetch the next page:

```bash
curl "https://api.moneydev.com.br/v1/transactions?cursor=NEXT_CURSOR_VALUE" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SECRET_KEY"
```

## Fees Overview

MoneyDev charges a blended fee composed of a **percentage rate** plus a **fixed amount per transaction**. The table below shows illustrative default rates:

| Payment Method | Rate | Fixed Fee |
|---|---|---|
| PIX | 0.99% | R$0,00 |
| Boleto | 1.49% | R$1,99 |
| Credit Card (1×) | 2.99% | R$0,49 |
| Credit Card (2–6×) | 3.49% | R$0,49 |
| Credit Card (7–12×) | 3.99% | R$0,49 |
| Debit Card | 1.99% | R$0,49 |

<Note>
The rates above are **illustrative only**. Your actual fees depend on your negotiated contract with MoneyDev. Log in to the Dashboard and navigate to **Configurações → Taxas** to view the exact rates applied to your account.
</Note>

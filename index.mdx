## Get Up and Running

Follow these four steps to go from zero to accepting live payments with MoneyDev.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [dashboard.moneydev.com.br](https://dashboard.moneydev.com.br) to create your MoneyDev account. The process takes less than two minutes — no credit card required to start testing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Your API Keys">
    After signing in, navigate to **Dashboard → API Keys**. You'll find a pair of keys: a test key (prefixed `sk_test_`) for development and a live key (prefixed `sk_live_`) for production. Copy your test key to start building.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Your First Payment">
    Send a `POST` request to `https://api.moneydev.com.br/v1/payments` with your API key in the `Authorization` header and a JSON body describing the charge. The [Quick Start](/quickstart) guide walks you through a complete PIX payment example.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go Live">
    Once you've tested your integration in the sandbox, swap your `sk_test_` key for your `sk_live_` key, complete the account verification in the dashboard, and you're ready to accept real payments from Brazilian customers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  MoneyDev provides a full **sandbox environment** for testing. All requests made with a `sk_test_` API key are processed in the sandbox — no real money moves and no actual PIX or boleto transactions are created. Use the sandbox freely during development before switching to your live key.
</Note>

Boleto bancário is one of Brazil's most widely used payment methods. Unlike cards or PIX, boleto does not require a bank account or credit card — customers can pay at any bank branch, lottery shop (casa lotérica), ATM, or through internet banking. This makes it an essential option for reaching unbanked customers, older demographics, and B2B transactions where invoice-based payment is standard practice. MoneyDev generates boletos that are valid at all major Brazilian banks and payment networks.

## How boleto works with MoneyDev

After you create a boleto through the API, MoneyDev returns a barcode number and a hosted boleto URL that you can share with your customer. The customer prints the boleto or accesses it digitally, then pays at any authorized location before the due date. Payment confirmation arrives **D+1** (one business day after payment) via a `payment.paid` webhook.

## Creating a boleto payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the payment">
    Send a `POST` request to `/payments` with `method` set to `"boleto"`. Boleto requires a full customer address, a due date, and optionally custom payment instructions that will be printed on the boleto itself.

    ```bash
    curl --request POST \
      --url https://api.moneydev.com.br/v1/payments \
      --header 'Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SECRET_KEY' \
      --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
      --data '{
        "method": "boleto",
        "amount": 25000,
        "currency": "BRL",
        "description": "Invoice #2024-089",
        "due_date": "2024-12-31",
        "instructions": "Não aceitar após o vencimento. Pagável em qualquer banco.",
        "customer": {
          "name": "Carlos Oliveira",
          "email": "carlos.oliveira@empresa.com.br",
          "cpf": "987.654.321-00",
          "address": {
            "street": "Rua das Flores",
            "number": "142",
            "district": "Centro",
            "city": "São Paulo",
            "state": "SP",
            "zip_code": "01310-100"
          }
        }
      }'
    ```

    The `amount` is in **centavos** — `25000` equals R$ 250,00. The `due_date` must be in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share the boleto URL or barcode with your customer">
    MoneyDev returns a `boleto` object containing both the barcode string and a hosted PDF URL. Send the URL to your customer by email or display it on your confirmation page.

    ```json
    {
      "id": "pay_7rQmLvB2nKwJ",
      "status": "waiting_payment",
      "method": "boleto",
      "amount": 25000,
      "currency": "BRL",
      "boleto": {
        "barcode": "34191.09008 61207.727308 71140.903483 1 94190000025000",
        "boleto_url": "https://api.moneydev.com.br/v1/payments/pay_7rQmLvB2nKwJ/boleto.pdf",
        "due_date": "2024-12-31",
        "instructions": "Não aceitar após o vencimento. Pagável em qualquer banco."
      }
    }
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customer pays at a bank, lottery shop, or app">
    Your customer can pay the boleto through any of the following channels before the due date:

    - Any bank branch or ATM
    - Lottery shops (casas lotéricas)
    - Internet banking or mobile banking apps
    - Payment apps that support boleto scanning (e.g., Nubank, Mercado Pago, PicPay)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive the payment.paid webhook">
    After the customer pays, the Brazilian banking network processes the transaction and MoneyDev receives confirmation typically on the **next business day (D+1)**. When the payment is confirmed, MoneyDev sends a `payment.paid` event to your webhook endpoint.

    ```json
    {
      "event": "payment.paid",
      "payment_id": "pay_7rQmLvB2nKwJ",
      "amount": 25000,
      "currency": "BRL",
      "paid_at": "2024-12-28T10:15:00Z"
    }
    ```

    Only release goods or services **after** receiving and validating this webhook.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Response fields

<ResponseField name="boleto.barcode" type="string">
  The numeric barcode line (linha digitável) that customers can type into any internet banking portal or app to pay the boleto.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="boleto.boleto_url" type="string">
  A hosted URL for the printable boleto PDF. You can display this as a download link or send it directly to the customer by email.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="boleto.due_date" type="string (YYYY-MM-DD)">
  The date by which the customer must pay the boleto. Payments received after this date may be rejected or subject to late fees.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="boleto.instructions" type="string">
  Custom instructions printed on the boleto. Use this to communicate payment terms, late fee policies, or contact information.
</ResponseField>

## Late fees

You can optionally include `fine` and `interest` fields in the request body to automatically apply late fees to overdue boletos:

```json
{
  "method": "boleto",
  "amount": 25000,
  "currency": "BRL",
  "due_date": "2024-12-31",
  "fine": {
    "percentage": 2.0
  },
  "interest": {
    "percentage": 1.0
  }
}
```

| Field | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| `fine.percentage` | float | One-time penalty applied after the due date (e.g., `2.0` for 2%). |
| `interest.percentage` | float | Monthly interest rate applied to overdue boletos (e.g., `1.0` for 1% per month). |

Both fields are optional. If omitted, no late fees are applied.

<Warning>
  Boleto payments **do not confirm instantly**. After a customer pays, settlement takes **1 to 2 business days** depending on the payment channel used. Do not release orders or services based on the customer's claim of payment — always wait for the `payment.paid` webhook from MoneyDev before fulfilling the order.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The `due_date` must be formatted as `YYYY-MM-DD` and must be set to a **maximum of 60 days** from the creation date. Boletos with a due date beyond 60 days are not permitted by Brazilian banking regulations. If you need longer payment terms, create the boleto closer to the intended due date.
</Note>

MoneyDev is a Brazilian payments gateway that lets businesses of all sizes accept the payment methods Brazilians actually use — PIX, boleto bancário, and credit and debit cards — through a single, developer-friendly REST API. Instead of integrating with multiple acquirers and banks, you connect once to MoneyDev and get access to Brazil's entire payment ecosystem, complete with real-time webhooks, a full-featured dashboard, and transparent, flat-rate pricing.

## How the API Works

MoneyDev exposes a standard **REST API** that communicates exclusively over HTTPS, with **JSON** request and response bodies. Requests authenticate with an OAuth 2.0 access token obtained via the [client credentials grant](/api-reference/authentication).

**Base URL**

```text
https://api.moneydev.com.br
```

Authentication and service endpoints sit at the root (`/oauth/token`, `/health`); business resources — customers, products, offers, and checkouts — live under `/v1`. URLs are resource-oriented and use standard verbs:

| Verb     | Use                                      |
|----------|------------------------------------------|
| `GET`    | Retrieve a resource or list resources    |
| `POST`   | Create a new resource                    |
| `PATCH`  | Update an existing resource              |

HTTP status codes reflect the outcome: `2xx` for success, `4xx` for client errors (bad request, authentication failure, validation), and `5xx` for server-side issues. Error responses include a machine-readable `code`, a human-readable `message`, and a `request_id`. See the [errors reference](/api-reference/errors).

## Who MoneyDev Is For

MoneyDev is designed to serve any business that sells to customers in Brazil.

**E-commerce stores** use MoneyDev to offer the complete set of Brazilian payment methods at checkout — reducing cart abandonment by letting customers pay the way they prefer, whether that's scanning a PIX QR code, printing a boleto, or paying in installments on a credit card.

**SaaS companies** rely on MoneyDev's recurring billing engine to manage subscriptions, collect monthly or annual plan fees in BRL, and handle failed payments automatically with smart retry logic.

**Marketplaces and platforms** build on MoneyDev's split payment infrastructure to collect a transaction from a buyer and instantly route funds to the correct seller, minus the platform's commission — without writing custom ledger code.

If your business collects money from people in Brazil, MoneyDev gives you the infrastructure to do it reliably, securely, and at scale.
